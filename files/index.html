July 4, 1776

The Continental Congress adopted the Declaration of Independence, formally announcing the colonies' break from British rule.

The First Fireworks

John Adams predicted the day would be marked with "illuminations" — fireworks first lit the sky over the holiday just one year later, in 1777.

13 Stripes, 50 Stars

The stripes honor the original thirteen colonies; each star represents one of the fifty states standing together today.
